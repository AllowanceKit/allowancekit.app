Features

Everything a family needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

No bank connections. No debit cards. No real money transfers. Just a virtual tracker that teaches kids to manage their allowance.

Set it and forget it

Choose an amount, pick a schedule, and AllowanceKit deposits automatically every week, fortnight, or month. Even catches up if you forget to open the app.

Save, spend, give

Split every deposit into custom buckets like Save, Spend, and Give. Teach your kids the basics of budgeting without a single lecture.

Kid's own view

Kids check their balance on their own device or the family iPad. Big, friendly numbers. Read-only. Optional passcodes keep siblings out.

Syncs everywhere

iCloud keeps everything in sync across all your devices. Add money on your iPhone, and it shows up on the family iPad instantly.

Truly private

All data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. No servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. We literally cannot see your data.

Virtual tracking only

AllowanceKit is a ledger, not a bank. No bank connections, no card numbers, no real money moves through the app. You handle the cash, we handle the math.
